Last Chapter : Why is Hindi Commentary Criticized??
Hindi Commentary ko lekar aaj jo hum criticism dekhte hai woh koi random
criticism nahi hai. Balki iske pure 5 major factors hai jiski vajah se Hindi
Commentary is getting rotten.
1) Overuse of Slang , shayari and wordplay
Hindi commentary ko lekar jo complaint sabse jayda dikhti hai woh hai
commentary choodke baki sab bol dena. Jaise koi cricket match nahi balki koi
shayri muishara chal raha ho. Jisme sabse jyada critiscice kara jata hai former
Indian opener “Navjot SIngh Sidhu”. Jitne Navjot singh siddhu ne apne career
mein run nahi banaye hai usse jyada toh unhe social media platform pe apni
commentary ke liye galiyan pad chuki hai.
"Gharwali ki tarah pitch ka mood badal sakta hai."
"Ball lagi Ram ko, ghayal hua Shyam."
Yeh kuch phrases hai jo aksar Sidhu apni commentary mein use kar dete hai.
Ab in baato ka cricket se toh choodo ek normal context se bhi koi naata nahi
hai. Yeh baate ek-2 baar viewer sehen kar sakta hai but aisi baate hi jab
commentary ban jaye toh us commentator aur style ka kuch nahi ho sakta .
Aur yeh ek baar nahi hai , balki pichle IPL ke dauran jab Sidhu ne commentary
mein vapsi ki thi toh show ki TRP aur broadcasters ki vajah se unhone aisi
commentary kari ki unhe pure season criticism jhelna pada. Aur yeh baat sab
sirf Sidhu pe aake nahi khadi ho jati.

2) Reduced Technical Analysis
Ab jab commentary bigad hi gayi hai toh jo cheez Hindi commentary mein
pehle jaan bhar deti thi woh thi cricket ko leke analysis. Maano ya maano jitna
ek cricket fan cricket ko dekhkar us sport ko utna nahi samajhta hai balki
commentary sun sun ke samajh jaata hai. But jab commentary asisi jho rahi
ho toh analysis toh choodo , cricket ki normal baat ho jaye toh bhi bohot badi
baat hogi. Jaise pehle ke samay mein bowler ke bowling changes se lekar
captain ke mindset ki baate hoti thi, aaj vahi sab jokes aur one liner pe aake
reh gaye hai. IPL 2025 ke dauran hindi commentary ko lekar debate bhi
online tabhi shuru hui thi jab ek fan unke commentary mein kari hui analysis
pe sawaal utha diye the.
But lekin Hindi commentators analysis kar dete toh shayad unke
broadcasters ki reach woh numbers provide nahi pati jo tab kar rahi thi.
3) Personal Bias and Narrative Building
Ab yeh jo teesra point hai , this might bhi very controversial to u all , but jo
hamne past mein witness kiya hai mein woh usi ke absis pe bata rha hu. Agar
mein aapse puchu ki english commentary aur hindi commentary major kya
farak hai toh aap sabhi log → Analysis aur Jokes wala point bol doge , which is
absolutely true. But jo cheez uske baad aati hai woh hai “Commentators ka
personal agenda aur unka narrative building” . Past mein aise kai instances
hue hai jaha pe hame hindi commentators ka bias kafi jyada saaf saaf dekhne
ko mila hai rather than english commentators…

Kya Hindi commentary hamesha se aisi hi thi? Kya shuru se hi Hindi
commentary ka focus cricket se jyada dialogues, shayari aur entertainment
par tha? Sach kahu toh nahi. Agar hum thoda peeche jaakar dekhe, especially
early 2000s ke time par, toh Hindi commentary ka format kaafi alag tha. Us
waqt commentary ka main kaam hota tha viewer ko match ka context
samjhana. Pitch ka behaviour kya hai, bowler ka plan kya hai, batsman kis
strategy se khel raha hai — yeh sab cheeze properly discuss hoti thi. Arun Lal
jaise commentators Hindi commentary box mein hote the toh woh calmly
field placements explain karte the. Maninder Singh spin bowling ke angles
aur variations par baat karte the. Ravi Shastri jab Hindi mein bolte the toh
unka tone bhi analytical hi hota tha. Matlab language Hindi thi…lekin content
pure cricket ka hota tha. Isliye agar aaj koi yeh bole ki Hindi commentary
hamesha se kharab thi, toh woh completely sahi baat nahi hai. Problem
language mein kabhi nahi thi…problem dheere dheere commentary ke style
change hone mein thi.
Aur shayad sabse important baat bhi yahi hai ki problem Hindi language nahi
hai. Problem commentary ka format hai. Agar aap dhyaan se dekho toh Hindi
commentators jab TV commentary se bahar YouTube shows ya analysis
videos karte hai, tab woh kaafi solid cricket breakdown dete hai. Aakash
Chopra ke YouTube analysis videos ho ya Vivek Razdan ke tactical discussions
— waha par pitch conditions, bowling plans aur match situations sab explain
kiye jaate hai. Matlab clearly cricket knowledge ki koi kami nahi hai. Lekin jab
wahi log live broadcast commentary box mein baithte hai, tab unhe ek aisa
format follow karna padta hai jaha entertainment ko zyada importance di
jaati hai. Aur yaha par broadcasters ka role bhi aata hai. Hindi commentary
actually duniya ka sabse zyada watched cricket commentary feed hai, isliye
broadcasters usse aise design karte hai ki woh maximum audience ko engage
kare — especially casual viewers ko jo detailed analysis se zyada excitement
aur reactions dekhna pasand karte hai. Toh agar honestly dekha jaye toh yeh
Hindi vs English ka debate utna simple nahi hai jitna internet par lagta hai.
Real debate actually analysis vs entertainment ka hai. Aur jab tak
commentary ka focus cricket ko explain karne se zyada viral moments aur
punchlines banane par rahega, tab tak Hindi commentary par criticism aata
rahega.
