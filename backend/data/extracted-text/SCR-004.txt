INTRODUCTION
Cricket history mein kai naam aise hai joki records ke saath likhe jaate hai,
jaise Rohit Sharma ke 3 double 100s , Virat ke fastest 10k ODI runs aur ya
phir Sachin Tendulkar ka anginat records. But jaha pe ek taraf cricket
legends pe baat unke cricketing career ke upar ki jaati hai, vahi pe kuch
cricketers aate hai jinki baat sirf controversies mein kari jaati hai. Jinme se aaj
ham baat karne wale hai India ke former opener , ek massive six hitter and
abhi ke cricket commentator “Navjot Singh Siddhu” ki. Ek aise cricketer jinki
shuruwaat toh hui thi ek massive set back lekin dekhte hi dekhte na keval
unhone Indian cricket mein six hitting ka culture shuru kia balki usi ke
dauran woh aisi aisi controveries mein pade jinki actual ginti shayad kisi ko
pata hi nahi hai. Phir chahe woh unka khudka cricket career ko ya phir
retirement ke baad ki unki TV Life. Apne international career mein Jail jaane
se lekar Cricket ke baad bhi controversies mein kai baar Siddhu ka naam aya
hai. Aur aaj ki is video mein hum Navjot Singh Siddhu ki isi controversial life
ko dekhege ki jo player ek time pe Sachin Tendulkar ke world cup mein
opening partner the, woh 2025 aate aate ek ke baad ek controveries mein
phase chuke hai.
