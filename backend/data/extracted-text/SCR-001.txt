khatam hota hai aur England already 67 pe hi all out ho chuki thi . Aakhri din
aate aate England woh almost haanre wali thi jab 4th innings mein world

ki jo us test match ke baad hua woh aaj bhi test cricket ke itihaas mein baar
baar repeat karaya jaata hai. Yeh baat hai us iconic test knock jisne us match

PRE MATCH SCENARIO
Ab agar hum us match se pehle ke scenario ki baat karein, toh situation
England ke liye utni achhi nahi thi jitni log soch rahe the. England aur english
cricket ke liye sabse iconic event ho chuka tha jab kuch mahino pehle woh ek
dramatic manner mein 2019 ka world cup jeet ke aa rahi thi. England ke
itishaas ka pehla world cup aur us team ka confidence maano aasman choo
raha tha. But as we all know jaha baat aati hai test cricket ki………My friend
anything can happen in the deadliest format of the game. Aur jab saamne ho
Australia and dau pe ashes ki trophy lagi ho toh pressure aana toh laazmi hai..
Australia, jinke top players Steve Smith aur David Warner sandpaper bans ke
baad wapas aaye the, unka focus sirf ek cheez pe tha — Ashes jeetna. Kyuki
jitna critism unhone pichle ek saal mein jhela hai , even is tour ke starting
mein bhi jis tarike se English fan unhe boo karne wale the aur smith ko
sandpaper dikhake disrespect karne wale the. English fans had no idea ki woh
kin players ko uksa rahe hai. Because initial tests mein Smith no England ke
saath kiya hai, woh fans ko regret hi nahi balki ek liflong trauma mein daal
deta hai.
Pehla Test Edgbaston mein hua jaha Steve Smith ne apne comeback pe 144
runs ki ek shandaar paari kheli aur England ke bowlers ko poora thaka diya.
James Anderson, jo England ke sabse senior bowler the, match ke first day pe
hi injured ho gaye the, jiski vajah se yeh series ab England ke liye aur mushkil
hote hue dikh rahi thi. Is injury ne England ke bowling attack ko kaafi weak kar
diya. Australia ne woh Test jeet kar 1-0 ki lead le li jisme Smith ne ek aur baar
2nd inning mein century lagai. World cup winning side ab bas Steve Smith ke
redemption ka shikaar bante hue nazar aa rahi thi. Jitni umeed fans ne World
champions england se lagai thi , woh saari first test ke baad badalti hui dikh
rahi thi.
Doosra Test Lord’s mein tha and it was no different . Steve Smith aur Marnus
Labuchagne ne na keval ek sustaibale innings kheli, but us match ko jo
Australia almost haarne wali thi use draw kara diya. England ke batting
line-up, specially top order, poore series mein struggle kar rahe the. Jason
Roy, Joe Denly, aur Rory Burns consistent runs nahi bana pa rahe the. Jaha pe
ek taraf Joe Denly aur Rory Burns ne proper 20 test matches bhi nahi khele
the , vahi pe hamare white ball specialist Jason roy ki toh yeh pehli series thi.
Lekin phir series ka aata hai teesra Test Match. Headingley Leeds !
Ek match jisne us puri Ashes ko hi nahi balki Modern Ashes ke complete
legacy ko hi redifine karke rakh diya tha. Jise execute karne wale aur koi nahi
balki 2019 world cup final ke hero Ben stokes the.
Series ka third Test Headingley (Leeds) mein hona tha — aur yahaan England
ke paas ek hi option tha, jeet. Agar woh match haar jaate, toh Australia 2-0 se
aage jaata aur Ashes unke haath se nikal jaati. Pressure obviously bahut zyada
tha. Team ke andar bhi sabko pata tha ki agar yeh match haara toh criticism
aur badega.
Is time pe Ben Stokes ke upar sabse zyada expectation thi. World Cup final ke
baad unka image ek “national hero” jaisa ban gaya tha. Lekin Test cricket mein
woh form continue kar paayenge ya nahi, yeh sabko dekhna tha. Dressing
room ke andar bhi Ben Stokes sabse focused player dikh rahe the. Reports ke
mutabiq woh apne practice sessions mein sabse last tak nets mein rehete
the, aur poora concentration red-ball game pe shift kar rahe the.
Australia ki team ke paas ek solid pace attack tha — Cummins, Hazlewood,
aur Pattinson. Saath hi Labuschagne ne Smith ki jagah perfectly fill kar di thi.
Team ka confidence peak pe tha.
22 August 2019 ko jab Headingley Test start hua, tab dono teams ek alag
mindset ke saath utari. Australia ke liye yeh ek mauka tha Ashes retain karne
ka, aur England ke liye apni izzat bachane ka.
Match ke pehle hi day pe conditions cloudy thi, aur Headingley ka pitch
seamers ke liye perfect tha. Australia ne toss jeet kar batting choose ki, aur
England ne early wickets lene ki puri koshish kari. Jofra Archer ne
phenomenal bowling karte hue Australia ko 179 pe all-out kar diya. Lekin jaha
sabko laga ki ab England easy lead le lega, wahi team pehli innings mein sirf
67 pe all-out ho gayi.
Is moment ke baad pure England dressing room mein silence tha. Fans,
experts, sabko lag raha tha ki yeh Test match ab haath se gaya. But a certain
innings was about to come from a certain player….or NOT !
THE INNINGS !
Us match mein kuch important ho ya na ho lekin England ke recently bane
world cup star “Ben Stokes” ka chalna behad hi zaruri tha. Agar 2 matches ki
disappointment ke baad kisi player pe ashaaye lagaye jaa rahi thi, toh woh
keval Ben stokes hi the. Aur maano agle match hi woh English fans ki
umeedo par khade utre aur aisa perform kara, ki aaj tak bhi us performance ki
baat karte log nahi thakte.
Toh kahaani ki shuruwaat hoti hai 22 August 2019 se, Ashes ka teesra test
match aur Headingley (LEEDS) ke us ground pe dono teams test match
khelne utarti hai. Jaha pe Australia lead ko badane ke liye match khel rahi thi,
vahi England apne hi ghar par ijjat talash rahi thi. Test match ke shuru hote hi
England ne underperform karna shuru kar diya aur 80% of test match mein
Australia nee dominance dikhae rakha. Jaha Aus ne pehle innings mein keval
179 hi runs banai paayi thi, vahi England comeback karne ki bajaye mahez 67
runs banakar all out ho gayi. Match ka chautha din aate aate jo English crowd ,
England team ki comeback ki umeed kar rahe the, ab bas kai sawalon ke
saath hi bache the. Aur is sab ke zimmedaar Ben stokes ko hi banae jane
walla tha kyon ki surkhiyo mein toh vahi the. Agar is sab ke alawa baat kare
toh England us match ko almost haar chuki thi kyunki ab unhe 4th innings
match ko jeetne ke liye 359 runs chaiye the aur almost 1.5 day ka khel bacha
hua tha. Agar realistically baat kare toh Is sab ke baad ya toh england match
ko jeet ti ya phir direct haar ka saamna karna padta, kyon ki itna zyada time
ke baad draw ka koi option hi nahi tha. Lekin England tab bhi draw ke liye
khelti hai aur 77 over baad phas jaati hai. 77 over baad England ka score keval
159 hi hua tha aur 5 wicket gir chuke the. Sabko lag raha tha ki England draw
ke liye khel rahi hai is haalat ko dekh kar England match toh nahi jeetne wali.
Lekin us time pe crease pe ek batsman abhi bhi khada hua tha aaur woh the
“Ben stokes”. Bhale hi stadium mein baitha har ek aadmi England ki jeet ki
ummede kabki chood chuka ho, Lekin Ben stokes abhi bhi jeet ke liye khel
rahe the aur aisi situation mein jaha England kabhi bhi all out hoke match ko
haar sakti hai. Aur yahi hame Ben Stokes ki mental toughness batati hai ki ek
cricketer mentally kitna tough ho sakta hai ki jab stadium mein choodo, pure
tv media yeh maanke baith chuki thi ki England is point se kabhi bhi match

Match ka thrilling moment tab shuru hua jab match ke 116ve over mein Stuart
